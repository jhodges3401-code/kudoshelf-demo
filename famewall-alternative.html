FAQ

Is Kudoshelf a one-time payment?

Yes. $79 once for the Pro template. Hosting on GitHub Pages is free for public repos.

Is there a free version?

Yes. Kudoshelf Lite is MIT-licensed on GitHub.

Is Kudoshelf affiliated with Famewall?

No. It's an independent product built from scratch.
